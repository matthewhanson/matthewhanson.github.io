Matthew Hanson

Remote sensing · Open-source geospatial

I work on remote sensing and open-source geospatial software at LGND, building the infrastructure that makes satellite imagery findable — lately by way of embeddings rather than metadata.

I’ve spent most of the last decade on STAC, the SpatioTemporal Asset Catalog specification, and serve on its Project Steering Committee. Based in Dover, New Hampshire.
